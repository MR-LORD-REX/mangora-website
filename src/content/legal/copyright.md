Mangora is a reader application. It hosts no manga, manhwa, comics or galleries, and it stores no content of
its own. It displays pages fetched live from third-party providers that you choose.

## Takedown requests

Because the content belongs to, and is served by, the providers — not Mangora — a takedown request belongs
with the provider that hosts the material. Each provider publishes its own contact and policy.

## The Mangora project

Mangora's own name, logo and source code are the property of the project and its contributors, released under
the terms stated in the project repository. Screenshots on this site show the app's own interface only; no
third-party artwork appears here.

## Trademarks

Provider names and logos belong to their respective owners and are shown for identification only. Their
presence does not imply any partnership or endorsement.
